WALKING SIMULATOR - DOWNLOADED ASSETS AND AUDIO INVENTORY
Environmental audio, props and interaction polish pass, 2026-10-23
Unity 2019.4.4f1 / URP 7.3.1 / Scene: Assets/Scenes/The Game.unity

All assets below were downloaded during this pass. Every one is CC0 (public domain dedication):
no attribution is legally required, commercial use and modification are allowed.
Licenses were checked on each asset's own source page at download time.

License links
- Poly Haven: CC0 1.0 - https://polyhaven.com/license (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Freesound (CC0 sounds): CC0 1.0 - https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Existing Poly Haven license notice: Assets/Texture Maps/Poly Haven/LICENSE.txt

Common modifications
- Poly Haven models: downloaded as glTF (1K textures), meshes joined, decimated in Blender 5.1, pivot set to
  bottom centre, exported as FBX. The ARM texture (AO/roughness/metal) was converted into a Unity
  MetallicSmoothness map (R = metal, A = 1 - roughness). Albedo and OpenGL normal maps are unchanged copies.
  In Unity: URP/Lit material, MeshFilter/MeshRenderer, Box or Sphere collider, Rigidbody (mass 1) for pickups,
  tag Interact, PickupImpactAudio.
- Freesound sounds: trimmed, converted to mono 16-bit WAV, short fades, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS with ffmpeg.
  Unity import: Force To Mono, Decompress On Load, PCM.

SUMMARY
======================================================================
Total audio files downloaded: 15 (11 used, 4 not imported). 17 edited clips imported into Unity.
Total 3D models downloaded: 10 (8 used, 2 not in the project).
Total textures downloaded: 30 (3 per model: diffuse, OpenGL normal, ARM). 24 used, converted into 8 albedo,
  8 normal and 8 MetallicSmoothness maps.

Existing assets reused without downloading:
- Wooden Barrel Impact 1-7.ogg (Assets/Audio/SFX/Impacts) for the Wooden Chest Crate and Wooden Pail.
- Resources/Audio/metalImpact.wav for the Rusted Spade, Metal Toolbox, CameraModel impacts and the Crown.
- Resources/Audio/paperOpen.wav as the Newspaper pickup sound.
- Existing PickupImpactAudio, GrabRotate, SfxAudioSource, LowerSpikes and CandleExtinguishSequence systems.
- The three existing cabin chairs (polySurface28 in Cabin, Cabin (1), Cabin (2)) were made pickable, reusing the
  Wooden Barrel Impact clips. All pickable objects are grouped under the scene root "Pickup Objects".
- Existing barrels (5), (7), (8) were moved from the start area to cabin exteriors instead of being deleted.

Planned assets not obtained, and why:
- Wheelbarrow: no free model with a clear license was found (Poly Haven has none; Unity Asset Store and other
  marketplace options are paid or have unclear licenses).
- Teddy bear: no free model with a clear license was found; replaced by the Dirty Child Ball (user decision).
- Rusty metal bucket: no free model found; replaced by the Wooden Pail.
- Padlock material: the scene has no padlock objects, so this item was skipped (user decision).
- Glass impact sound for the whisky bottles: not downloaded; the bottles keep their current behaviour.

No asset with an unverified source, author or license was imported.
